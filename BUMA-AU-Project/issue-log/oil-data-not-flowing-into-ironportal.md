## SLA Notes

- Classified as **P2** under Schedule 3 §4.1. Initial read treated this as P3 on the assumption that BUMA seeing the raw lab email was an adequate workaround — but the critical function actually blocked is the **intervention action in IronPortal**, not just visibility of the sample data. This sample's Eval is **C** (copper high), which is meant to trigger an intervention action in IronPortal; while the data isn't flowing in, BUMA cannot raise that intervention through the normal process. Reading the number off an email doesn't substitute for that — there's no reasonable workaround for the actual impaired function, so P2 applies.
- P2 clock runs **continuous** (24/7, weekends included) — not business-hours-only like P3/P4.
- Formal clock to BukitTech starts at the first email addressed to them: 2026-08-17 04:50 AM.
- Response time: 2026-08-17 04:50 AM (raised) → 05:47 AM (acknowledged) = ~57 min.
- Note: Marius's internal complaint (2026-08-16 05:19 AM) references "nearly 24 hrs" from the 2026-08-15 06:38 AM lab email — that ~22–24h gap is the underlying delay being complained about, but it predates the formal raise to BukitTech and isn't part of the SLA clock above.
